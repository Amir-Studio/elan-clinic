01

CONSULTATIONS & SERVICES

How do I choose the right service?

Start with an initial conversation and an in-person assessment by a qualified professional. Your needs and expectations can then be discussed, along with suitable options, limitations and aftercare. A service should never be chosen based only on advertising or general recommendations.

Should I have a consultation first?

Yes. Your individual circumstances, allergy history, skin condition and expectations should be reviewed. After assessment, a specialist may advise against a service or suggest another option.

What happens during a consultation?

A consultation usually covers your goals and concerns, relevant background, available options, limitations, before- and aftercare, and possible costs. The exact process depends on the service and the clinic's actual procedures.

02

SAFETY & AFTERCARE

Are results the same for everyone?

No. Results vary from person to person and depend on many factors. No specific outcome can be guaranteed without an individual assessment. Discuss realistic expectations with a qualified professional.

What if I have sensitive skin?

Before making a decision, tell the specialist about allergies, previous skin reactions, and any products or medicines you use. Avoid starting new treatments or products without advice suited to your circumstances.

How is aftercare explained?

Aftercare instructions should be provided by a qualified professional and tailored to the service and your individual needs. Ask about follow-up timing and how to contact the clinic if anything is unclear.

03

COSTS, TIMING & BOOKINGS

How are service costs determined?

Costs depend on the service, individual circumstances and professional assessment. The final price and what it includes should be explained clearly before you decide. This demo website does not list real prices.

How can I book a consultation?

Use the request form on the home page to select a topic and preferred contact time. The current form is for demonstration only and does not send or store information. Official clinic contact details must be added for real bookings.

Can I change my appointment?

Once a booking system is available, contact the clinic through its official channel with your appointment details. Cancellation and rescheduling terms should be clearly provided when booking.

04

INFORMATION & PRIVACY

Where does form information go?

In this version, the form is not connected to a server and no data is sent or stored. Before launch, secure infrastructure, a privacy policy, data-retention periods and access procedures must be established and explained.

Are the team profiles and photos real?

No. This website is a design concept. The doctor photographs in the team section are placeholders and do not represent actual clinic staff. Replace them with authorised photographs and verified names, qualifications and biographies before public use.

Does this page replace medical advice?

No. This content is general guidance about visiting the clinic and is not a diagnosis or personal medical advice. Consult a qualified doctor or specialist about your own circumstances.
